Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

QRLY is built to make QR code creation simple, useful, and respectful of your information. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and what you can expect when using QRLY.

01

Overview

Welcome to QRLY. By using this website and its QR code generation tools, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

QRLY is designed primarily as a browser-based utility. Our goal is to minimize unnecessary data collection while providing a reliable and useful QR code generation experience.

Privacy-minded by design

QR generation is performed in your browser whenever possible, reducing the need to send QR content to a remote server.

02

Information we may collect

QRLY does not require you to create an account simply to generate a QR code. Depending on how you interact with the website, certain information may nevertheless be processed.

Information you provide

If you contact us through the contact form, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message subject
  • The contents of your message

Technical information

Basic technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics, or third-party services used to operate and improve the website. This can include information such as browser type, device type, approximate usage information, and technical logs.

03

QR codes and generated data

QRLY allows you to enter information such as URLs, text, email addresses, phone numbers, Wi-Fi details, and contact information to create QR codes.

For normal QR generation, this information is processed by the browser to construct the QR code. The QR content is not inherently sent to QRLY's servers simply because you generated a QR code.

Your QR content stays with your workflow

QRLY's client-side generator is designed so that ordinary QR creation can happen directly in your browser.

Keep in mind that once a QR code is scanned, the information encoded inside it may be accessible to the person or device performing the scan. Avoid placing sensitive information into QR codes unless you understand the risks involved.

04

How information is used

Information processed through QRLY may be used for legitimate purposes connected to operating and improving the website.

  • To provide and maintain QRLY's tools and website.
  • To respond to messages and support requests.
  • To identify and resolve technical problems.
  • To improve usability, performance, and reliability.
  • To protect the website against abuse, spam, or security threats.

We do not use the information you enter into the QR generator as a reason to build a personal advertising profile about you.

05

Contact form submissions

When you send a message through the QRLY contact form, your submission is processed through our form service provider, Formspree.

This means information included in your message, including your name, email address, subject, and message, may be transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver the submission.

We use this information to review and respond to your inquiry. We do not ask you to submit passwords, payment information, government identification, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the contact form.

06

Third-party services

QRLY may rely on third-party services to provide certain website functionality, infrastructure, or communication features.

These services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Formspree — used to process contact form submissions.
  • CDN providers — may deliver libraries, icons, fonts, or other website assets.
  • Hosting providers — may process technical request information required to serve the website.

Third-party services are selected based on their usefulness and reliability, but their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

07

Cookies and similar technologies

QRLY is designed to work without requiring an account or unnecessary cookies for its core QR generation functionality.

However, third-party services integrated into the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics, or service delivery.

Your browser also provides controls that allow you to manage or block cookies according to your preferences.

08

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through QRLY. However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, avoid submitting confidential or highly sensitive information through QRLY's contact form or encoding such information into publicly accessible QR codes.

09

Your choices

You have control over the information you choose to provide to QRLY.

  • You can use the QR generator without creating an account.
  • You can choose not to submit optional information.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can contact us if you have questions about a message or information you submitted.
10

Children's privacy

QRLY is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us through the website, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to QRLY, our services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Still have a question?

If something about this policy is unclear, we'd rather you ask. Send us a message and we'll do our best to clarify how QRLY handles information.
