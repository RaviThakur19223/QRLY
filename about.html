The idea

QR codes are simple.
Using them should be too.

QR codes are everywhere — on menus, packaging, business cards, posters, products and screens.

But many QR tools make the process feel unnecessarily complicated. Too many settings, too many distractions and too much friction between an idea and the final result.

QRLY takes the opposite approach. We focus on the things people actually need: choose what you're sharing, customize it when you want to, and download a QR code that is ready to use.

No complicated workflow. No unnecessary account creation. Just a clean tool built for the web.
